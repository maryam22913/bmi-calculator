Why Children Need "BMI Percentiles"

Baro (adults) ka BMI fixed hota hai (jaise 18.5 se 24.9 normal hota hai). Lekin bachay aur teens musalsal barh rahe hote hain, aur unka body fat umar (age) aur jins (boy/girl) ke mutabiq tabdeel hota hai.

Is liye CDC aur WHO bacho ke liye Percentile System use karte hain, jisme unke wazan aur qad ka mawazna hazaro doosre hum-umar bacho se kiya jata hai:

< 5th Percentile: Underweight (Wazan kam hai)
5th to 84th Percentile: Healthy Weight (Bilkull Sehat Mand)
85th to 94th Percentile: Overweight (Wazan barh raha hai)
≥ 95th Percentile: Obesity (Ziada wazan, doctor se mashwara zaroori)
🥦

Healthy Parenting Tips for Growing Kids

Agar aapke bache ka BMI percentile normal se bahar hai, to foran crash dieting ya wazan kam karne ki sakhti harghiz na karein, kyu ke unki haddiya aur zehni salahiyat barh rahi hoti hai.

  • Outdoor Play: Rozana kam az kam 60 minutes physical activity ya sports.
  • Limit Screen Time: Mobile aur TV ka waqt mehdood karein taake physical mobility barhe.
  • Healthy Home Meals: Soft drinks aur junk food ki jagah doodh, taaza phal, anda aur home-cooked meals dein.
  • Consult Pediatrician: Kisi bhi baray faislay se pehle bacho ke doctor se unke growth curve ka checkup karwayein.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about BMI calculations and healthy weight management.

For adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 kg/m² is considered the healthy sweet spot. In this range, the risk of developing heart disease, diabetes, and hypertension is at its absolute lowest.

Screen ke ooper page header par "Children & Teens (2–19 Years)" button par click karein. Ye mode pediatric growth percentiles aur bacho ke hisab se calculations activate kar deta hai!

Ideal body weight is calculated using your height: Minimum healthy weight = 18.5 × [height in meters]² and Maximum healthy weight = 24.9 × [height in meters]². This gives your personal healthy kg range.

Athletes have a high amount of lean muscle tissue. Since muscle is heavier and denser than fat, an athlete may have a high BMI while having very low body fat percentage.
